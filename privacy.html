Privacy policy

JARVIS · last updated 24 October 2026

JARVIS is a personal assistant that runs on its owner's own computer for a single user: its owner. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else's data is collected.

What Google data JARVIS accesses

  • Google Drive, Docs and Sheets (read-only): to search and read the owner's files when the owner asks about them.
  • Gmail (compose), Google Calendar (events) and Google Sheets (edit), only if the owner opts in: to create a Gmail draft, add a calendar event or update a spreadsheet. Each action is shown to the owner first and happens only after they approve it. JARVIS never sends email; drafts are sent by the owner from Gmail.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to answer the owner's own requests.
  • The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer.
  • When the owner asks about a document, its text may be sent to the AI model provider that powers JARVIS (Anthropic) to produce the answer. It is not used for advertising, sold or shared with anyone else.
  • JARVIS does not keep copies of Google files beyond what the owner chooses to save in their own notes.

Google API Services User Data Policy

JARVIS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect Google at any time in JARVIS's settings, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting deletes the stored token.

Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on JARVIS's Google sign-in screen.
