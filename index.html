JARVIS

A private, single-user assistant that runs on its owner's own computer.

JARVIS helps one person organise their tasks, projects, notes and files. It is not a public service: it runs locally on its owner's PC and only its owner signs in to it.

When its owner connects their Google account, JARVIS can:

  • search and read their Google Drive, Docs and Sheets (read-only by default);
  • if the owner separately allows it, prepare Gmail drafts, add calendar events and update Google Sheets, each one only after the owner explicitly approves it.

Read the privacy policy.

JARVIS is a personal project and is not affiliated with Google.
